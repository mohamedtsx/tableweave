one actual scenario), not28 invented extra passes:

  epoch or interfere with a current writer.
- Foreign preflight conflict rejects before poison reflection/observers. Competing
  descriptor admission is rechecked without overwriting/revoking the winner.

  released competitors permit ordinary later serial admission.

  remain exact. **Positive** plain helper with P2 and equal P1 origin may pass; the
  exact binding still says P2, establishing the separate future expected-P check.

  distinct real controller publications remain independent.
