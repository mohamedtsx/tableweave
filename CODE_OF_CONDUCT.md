# Community conduct

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive and inclusive. Critique
ideas and code, not identities. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized
conduct, doxxing and publishing others' private information are unacceptable.
Respect contributor boundaries, attribution and licensing. Disagreement does not
justify personal attacks or pressure for unpaid work.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, request corrections, limit participation
or exclude participants as appropriate to severity/repetition, while respecting
privacy and giving a fair opportunity to respond where safe. This applies in
project spaces and when representing the project publicly; it promises no staffed
moderation SLA or legal adjudication.

**Reporting readiness:** a private conduct-reporting channel and responsible
maintainer have not yet been configured. Ask the project maintainer for a private
route without posting incident details, identities or private evidence publicly.
Do not fabricate a reporting email. Establish and verify the channel before
public distribution; see [publication inputs](docs/operations/releasing.md#licensing-and-publication-inputs).
